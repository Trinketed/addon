## Latest (2026-09)
- **Stream overlay** (TrinketedHistory, off by default — Settings tab, `/tko`, or `/trinketed overlay`): a compact viewer-facing panel showing recent rated results and a labeled session strip (`RECORD · NET · CR · MMR · STREAK`, streak always on). Rows lead with a solid W/L chip, show post-game CR + signed delta and both rosters as framed class icons (spec icons and spec badges optional) with a quiet `VS` separator; players who died are desaturated with a red corner mark. Rows with a replay open it on click (`CLICK TO REPLAY`, on by default; imported games aren't clickable — no replay to open) and everything else stays click-through. Bracket is a header fact driven by `BRACKET AUTO` (follows your last game; pin `2V2/3V3/5V5/ALL` in `/tko settings`); in ALL the CR/MMR tiles carry the last-played bracket in their labels. Locked = zero chrome and click-through except replay rows; unlocked = gold border, cog, corner scale grip. Visuals follow the design handoff (D1–D12): TrinketedLib fonts/palette at 92% surface alpha, new `lib.C` result tokens (`win/loss/winText/lossText/onChip`, aliases of the web `--success/--danger` family) and `lib.OVERLAY_ALPHA`. Settings live in `TrinketedHistoryDB.overlay`; the session strip's MMR is the player's own personal MMR (this realm reports per-player MMR, verified empirically — never a first-enemy-row value).

## 2026-08
- **Fix — Cooldowns stayed on screen after converting a party to a raid:** `ScanPartyMembers` has always torn down party bars when `IsInRaid()` and you're not in an arena, but every caller was gated behind the same tracking check that a raid makes false — so on raid conversion the teardown was skipped and the bars froze on screen until the next `/reload`. The roster and zone handlers now route into the teardown when raided. Arena inside a raid group still tracks normally, and the teardown clears the pet-owner map like arena exit already did.
- **Matches filters survive /reload:** the comp slot builders (both sides) and the bracket chip selection now persist to SavedVariables and restore on load; restored comps are re-clamped to the bracket exactly like a live edit. Season intentionally still re-defaults to the live season on panel open. "Open in Trinketed app" now also carries the active comp filters in the jump intent (`yourComp`/`enemyComp`: sorted class-slug list + includes/exactly verb, the webapp's yc/ec + yv/ev params) so the companion can rebuild the same library filter — keys are absent when no filter is active, and older companions ignore them.
- **Comp filters rebuilt as a slot builder** (Matches tab, filter row 1): bracket chips (2v2/3v3/5v5/ALL) with live counts predicting what each bracket would show under the current filters, then both teams as always-visible slots. Click an empty slot for a class flyout (it keeps filling that side's next slot until the team is full), click a filled slot to remove it. Empty slots are wildcards — a partially filled team means "any team including these", a full team means "exactly this roster"; no mode toggle exists. Matches the Trinketed webapp's comp bar one for one, so filtering feels identical in-game and on the site. Replaces the Player Comp / Enemy Comp / Bracket dropdowns; Partner moves right on the same row.
- **Comp names and class icons in lockstep with the app:** match rows show class icons beside every player name, and comp abbreviations (RD, DPR, WD, RMP…) come from the same curated registry the Trinketed app uses — the data table is generated from the app's canonical registry, so the two can never drift.
- **Character identity on saved games:** matches now record the recording character's realm and GUID (`playerRealm`, `playerGuid`) alongside the existing realm-stripped name. This lets Trinketed's library filter by character — and tell apart two characters that share a name on different realms. No in-game UI change; only games recorded after this update carry the new fields.
- **Open in Trinketed:** clicking a row on the Matches tab now opens a flyout with "Open in Trinketed app" and "Copy web link". The app option writes a jump intent to SavedVariables and reloads — the desktop companion picks it up on the flush, brings its window forward, and opens that game's review. The web option surfaces a `trinketed.com/goto` link pre-selected for Ctrl+C (WoW has no clipboard API). Dev mode keeps its game-id copy as a third entry. Without the companion running, the app option is a no-op reload; the web link works for everyone.

## 2026-07
- **Monorepo consolidation:** dropped the TrinketedCD/TrinketedHistory git submodules and vendored all modules as plain sibling folders; TrinketedAuras, TrinketedLC, and TrinketedLOS are now tracked in-repo but excluded from releases (`pkgmeta.yaml` ignore). Deleted the notify-parent/update-submodule workflow chain. Releases ship core + Cooldowns + History.
- **Enemies tab** in TrinketedHistory (Matches | Sessions | Teams | Enemies | Settings): every enemy faced with games/W-L/win%/net rating, sortable columns, name search; click an enemy to jump to the Matches tab filtered to all games against them
- **Free-text match search** on the Matches tab: space-separated terms all must match player names/classes/specs/races on either team or the map
- **Match share strings:** `/trinketed share [n]` exports a match (TRINKR1! prefix, LibDeflate); `/trinketed import` opens a pasted match directly in the replay viewer without adding it to your own history
- **Death recap** in the replay viewer: click a death in the feed for the victim's final 8 seconds — damage with real amounts and crit markers, effective healing, absorbs, misses, CC with DR category tags, and their own defensive casts
- **Queue timer:** chat prints how long the arena queue took when it pops (server-tracked wait time, survives /reload mid-queue)
- **Scoreboard capture:** per-player killing blows, deaths, damage, and healing are stored with each match
- **Safer data persistence:** replaced the old queue-time forced ReloadUI with an opt-out auto-reload that fires after zoning out of an arena (5s countdown, `/trinketed noreload` to cancel, never fires while queued or in combat), plus a Matches-tab nudge showing games not yet flushed to disk
- **Fix:** leaving the arena quickly after a win no longer records a LOSS (uses the already-detected winner)
- Vendored DRList-1.0 (DR tracking in replays) and wired it as a packager external; dynamic Welcome-tab module list driven by `RegisterSubAddon(desc=...)`; options panel now fires content OnShow on first open; removed TrinketedCD's dead legacy color picker; README/CLAUDE.md rewritten for the monorepo
